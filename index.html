Anahita Bolourani

AI Researcher · LLM Interpretability, Steering & Safety · RL for LLM Agents USC Institute for Creative Technologies · UCLA Trustworthy AI Lab · Ph.D., UCLA (2026)

Hi, I'm Anahita (Ana) Bolourani. I study what large language models represent inside, how we can steer them, and what that steering does to their safety. I earned my Ph.D. at UCLA in 2026, I'm finishing an M.S. in Statistics and Data Science there, and I do research at USC's Institute for Creative Technologies and UCLA's Trustworthy AI Lab.

My work has three threads:

  • Interpretability and steering. Locating where LLMs represent emotion, with probes and activation patching (Findings of ACL 2025), and co-developing PsySET (ACL 2026, oral), a benchmark for what steering emotion and personality does to safety and truthfulness.
  • Agents that know when to act. Training LLM agents with teacher distillation and PPO to decide when their evidence is enough to commit to a prediction (under review).
  • Efficient reasoning. TableRouter, a training-free method for answering questions over long tables at a fraction of the compute (under review).

I bring a statistician's habits to this work: calibrated metrics, leakage audits, and held-out tests fixed before the first model call. Before moving into AI, I worked on structural health monitoring and earthquake engineering.

News & Talks

ACL 2026 · Oral

Psychological Steering in LLMs (PsySET)

Annual Meeting of the ACL, Long Papers

Can we reliably steer an LLM's emotions and personality, and what does it cost? PsySET compares prompting, vector injection and fine-tuning, then checks safety, truthfulness, fairness and privacy. One finding: steering toward joy raised jailbreak success in Llama 3.1 8B Instruct.

Paper (ACL Anthology)
ICLR 2026 · Workshop

Sparks of Rationality

Workshop on Representational Alignment

Do reasoning LLMs choose like rational agents, and like people? Thinking models were more coherent on rational-choice axioms, yet that coherence coexisted with greater susceptibility to emotional steering.

Paper (arXiv)
COLM 2025 · Montreal

Impact of LLM Alignment on Impression Formation

Conference on Language Modeling (COLM)

Do language models form social impressions like humans do? Using Affect Control Theory, we show LLMs focus on the actor's identity while largely ignoring the behavior and the recipient, and alignment tuning doesn't fix it (sometimes it amplifies it).

ACL 2025

Mechanistic Emotion Inference in LLMs

Findings of ACL

If you're into LLM interpretability, inference-time steering, or cognitive modeling, see our paper! We localize how autoregressive LLMs infer emotions, show functional regions that encode appraisal-based representations, and causally steer generation by intervening on appraisal concepts, aligning outputs with theory and intuitive expectations.
